WHY IDENTITY GOVERNANCE MATTERS

Identity is the new security perimeter.

Gartner expects 75% of security failures to stem from inadequate identity management by 2026. Governing who has access to what — and why — is no longer optional; it is foundational to digital trust and regulatory compliance.

01

Regulation & compliance

GDPR, NIS2 and sector rules demand robust access controls and audit trails. Automated certification campaigns and compliance evidence cut manual audit effort by up to 70%.

02

Insider risk & least privilege

68% of breaches involve a human element — including privilege misuse. Least-privilege enforcement and continuous certification dramatically shrink the attack surface from within.

03

Lifecycle complexity

Employees, contractors and partners all need different access. Automated joiner-mover-leaver processes provision access on day one and revoke it completely on departure.
