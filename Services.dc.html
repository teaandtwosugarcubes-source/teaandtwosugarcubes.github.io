WHAT WE DO

End-to-end Saviynt IGA services

From architecture design to production deployment and ongoing operations — we deliver the full spectrum of identity services, with Saviynt Identity Cloud as the platform of choice.

01 — IMPLEMENTATION

IGA implementation & deployment

Full-cycle Saviynt Identity Cloud deployment for complex enterprise environments — from requirements gathering and architecture design through to production go-live and post-deployment support. Covering SCIM integration, REST API, RBAC, ABAC, and dynamic birthright rules.

SCIM REST API RBAC ABAC JML automation

02 — ONBOARDING

Application onboarding & connector development

Connecting your critical business systems to Saviynt using native connectors, custom REST API integrations, SOAP, SCIM, and MuleSoft middleware. Covering HR systems, ERP platforms, cloud providers, SaaS applications, and custom enterprise systems.

Active Directory Entra ID SAP SuccessFactors Workday Salesforce ServiceNow

03 — LIFECYCLE

Identity lifecycle automation

Designing and implementing joiner-mover-leaver processes driven by authoritative HR systems — ensuring access is provisioned accurately on day one, updated with every role change, and revoked completely upon departure. Includes dynamic user update rules and attribute-based provisioning.

Joiner-mover-leaver Birthright rules HR integration Auto-provisioning

04 — GOVERNANCE

Access governance & compliance

Implementing segregation-of-duties controls, scheduled certification campaigns, and access review workflows. Building actionable analytics for out-of-band access detection and high-risk entitlement monitoring — providing audit-ready compliance evidence.

SoD controls Certification campaigns Access reviews Analytics

05 — ADVISORY

IAM architecture & advisory

Strategic IAM advisory — including maturity assessments, Zero Trust identity architecture design, IGA product evaluation, and roadmap development. We help organisations build identity programs that scale with business growth and evolving threat landscapes.

Zero Trust IAM maturity BPMN process design Architecture design

06 — OPERATIONS

Managed support & BAU operations

Ongoing operational support for Saviynt environments — including incident resolution, configuration management, data refresh, UAT environment preparation, and knowledge transfer. We act as an extension of your team, keeping your IGA platform stable, current, and effective.

BAU support Incident management Knowledge transfer UAT preparation

ENTERPRISE IDENTITY CHALLENGES

The identity problems we solve.

Every organisation faces a spectrum of identity and access governance challenges. We help enterprises across the Nordics and EU diagnose and resolve these pain points — with precision, at enterprise scale.

Access sprawl & orphaned accounts

Unnecessary rights accumulating over time, and former employees' accounts left active — creating security risk and compliance exposure.

Manual provisioning bottlenecks

IT teams buried in manual access requests — slowing onboarding and introducing errors and security gaps in production.

Segregation-of-duties violations

Conflicting privileges that open the door to fraud or error — critical in finance, procurement, and ERP environments like SAP and Workday.

Audit & certification failures

Inability to produce timely, accurate access reports during audits — resulting in findings, remediation effort, and reputational risk.

Third-party & contractor risk

External identities managed ad hoc, without lifecycle controls or expiration policies — leaving persistent over-privileged accounts.

Multi-cloud & hybrid complexity

Identity fragmented across AD, Entra ID, AWS IAM, and SaaS — with no unified governance model or central visibility.

GDPR & NIS2 compliance gaps

Lack of demonstrable access-control processes required under GDPR and NIS2 — exposing organisations to penalties and enforcement.

Complex application onboarding

Legacy and modern systems that resist centralised IGA — non-standard APIs, bespoke data models, or missing native connectors.

Immature role engineering

No structured RBAC or ABAC framework — excessive manual entitlements, policy drift, and no demonstrable least privilege.
