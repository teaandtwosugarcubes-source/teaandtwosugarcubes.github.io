Why Identity Governance Matters

Identity Is the New Security Perimeter

Every organisation — from growing SMEs to global enterprises — faces mounting pressure to govern who has access to what, and why. According to Gartner, by 2026, 75% of security failures will result from inadequate identity management rather than external threats alone. IAM and IGA are no longer optional; they are foundational to digital trust and regulatory compliance.

Regulatory Compliance & GDPR

Organisations operating in the EU are subject to GDPR, NIS2, and sector-specific regulations requiring robust access controls and audit trails. Saviynt IGA provides the automated compliance evidence and certification campaigns that satisfy regulators and auditors — reducing manual effort by up to 70%.

Insider Threat Prevention

The 2024 Verizon Data Breach Investigations Report found that 68% of breaches involved a human element — including insider misuse and privilege abuse. Enforcing least-privilege access and continuous certification through Saviynt dramatically reduces the attack surface from within.

Workforce Complexity & Lifecycle Management

Modern enterprises manage permanent employees, contractors, seasonal workers, and external partners — all requiring different access profiles. Automated joiner-mover-leaver (JML) processes ensure access is provisioned accurately on day one and revoked promptly upon departure, eliminating orphaned accounts and access sprawl.

Cloud Transformation Risk

As organisations migrate to AWS, Azure, and multi-cloud environments, identity becomes the primary control point. KuppingerCole's Leadership Compass for IGA identifies Saviynt as an Overall Leader, specifically for its strength in cloud entitlement management and hybrid enterprise environments.

Audit Readiness & Segregation of Duties

Financial services, manufacturing, and public sector organisations face strict SoD (Segregation of Duties) requirements. Without automated SoD controls, a single misconfigured role can create significant fraud risk or audit findings. Saviynt IGA enforces SoD policies continuously, with real-time violation detection and remediation workflows.

Zero Trust Architecture

Gartner's Zero Trust Network Access research confirms that identity is the foundational pillar of Zero Trust. IGA provides the governance layer that ensures only authenticated, authorised, and continuously verified identities can access organisational resources — whether on-premises or in the cloud.

Enterprise Identity Challenges

The Identity Problems We Solve

Every organisation faces a spectrum of identity and access governance challenges. Spectrum Identity helps enterprises across the Nordic region and EU diagnose and resolve these critical pain points — with precision, and at enterprise scale.

Access Sprawl & Orphaned Accounts

Accumulation of unnecessary access rights over time and accounts belonging to former employees left active in systems — creating significant security risk and compliance exposure.

Manual Provisioning Bottlenecks

IT teams spending excessive time on manual access requests and approvals, slowing onboarding and increasing the risk of errors, inconsistencies, and security gaps in production environments.

Segregation of Duties Violations

Conflicting access privileges that create opportunities for fraud or errors — particularly critical in finance, procurement, and ERP environments including SAP and Workday.

Audit & Certification Failures

Inability to produce timely, accurate access reports during audits — resulting in regulatory findings, remediation effort, and reputational risk.

Third-Party & Contractor Access Risk

External identities — contractors, partners, suppliers — managed ad hoc without proper lifecycle controls or access expiration policies, leaving persistent over-privileged accounts.

Multi-Cloud & Hybrid Identity Complexity

Identity fragmented across on-premises Active Directory, Azure AD / Entra ID, AWS IAM, and SaaS applications — with no unified governance model or central visibility.

GDPR & NIS2 Compliance Gaps

Lack of demonstrable access control processes required under GDPR Article 5 and NIS2 obligations — exposing organisations to regulatory penalties and enforcement action.

Complex Application Onboarding

Difficulty integrating legacy and modern systems into a centralised IGA platform — due to non-standard APIs, bespoke data models, or absence of native connectors.

Lack of Role Engineering Maturity

Absence of a structured RBAC or ABAC framework — resulting in excessive manual entitlement assignments, policy drift, and difficulty demonstrating least-privilege compliance.
